## 1. Backlog Summary & Prioritization Framework

The remediation backlog translates technical audit findings into an actionable engineering queue. Issues are strictly prioritized based on their impact on candidate certification success and production workload execution:

- **P0 (Critical Corrections / Execution Blockers)**: Syntax errors in CLI commands, fatal Python exceptions, invalid SQL syntax, non-existent flags, and false "100% SLA" claims.
- **P1 (Important Technical Corrections)**: Missing IAM roles and service account bindings, operational race conditions, misleading SLA generalizations across editions, SDK syntax inconsistencies, and broken internal links.
- **P2 (Missing Content & Technical Currency)**: Outdated quotas and capacity ceilings, missing exam syllabus sub-bullets (prompting LLMs for query generation), non-standard idioms, and tracker classifications.
- **P3 (Editorial & Portability Enhancements)**: Script cross-platform portability (`sed -i`), question numbering consistency, and editorial precision.
